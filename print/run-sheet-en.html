Hair-cutting day: run sheet

Сэвлэг үргээх найр · Boy · About 50 guests · At home · You are the father and the MC

Date:   Child's хий нас:   Start time from the lama:

How to use this sheet. The times are an example for a 10:00 start. If your lama gives a different time, move every time by the same amount. The rule from the traditions: finish the hair cutting before noon. The blue boxes are what you say as the MC. Fill in the blank names before the day.

Ritual tray (ready by 08:30)

  • Scissors with a blue хадаг tied to the handle
  • A small wooden knife (a wooden butter knife is fine)
  • A bowl of fresh milk + about 60 small cups
  • Juniper (арц) or incense (хүж) + something to burn it on
  • A clean хадаг or pouch for the hair
  • The child's gift pouch (хишгийн уут)
  • Notebook and pen for the gift list
  • Electric clipper, fully charged
  • Blessing tickets in a bowl near the door
  • First-cutter card (page 3 of the tickets)
  • Цагаалга (rice cooked in milk, with аарц and raisins), made fresh
  • Airag, milk tea, juice. No vodka.
  • Thank-you gift for the first cutter; small gift bags for guest children

Rules almost every source agrees on

  • Cut the hair in the morning, before noon. Traditionally, guests who come after noon do not cut; they join the meal.
  • The first cut is at the right temple (for a boy).
  • Lead the child sunwise (clockwise) around the room.
  • Keep all the hair, also the clipper hair at the end. Never throw it away.
  • No vodka or hard alcohol. Airag is fine.
  • Food is cooked fresh on the day. No leftovers.
  • If an important relative cannot come, leave one lock uncut for them.

The day, step by step

Example times for a 10:00 start. The cutting ends before noon.

07:0090 min

Morning preparation

  • Cook the цагаалга and the meal fresh. Set the table. Put the ritual tray near the head of the table.
  • Charge the clipper. Put the blessing tickets in a bowl by the door.
  • Give the child a light breakfast. A rested child is the most important thing today.

Who: mother, kitchen helper, you

08:3020 min

Purify the scissors

  • Burn the juniper or incense. Pass the scissors through the smoke.
  • Tie the blue хадаг to the scissors handle.
  • Optional, if your family does it: an incense offering (сан тавих) and sprinkling the first of the milk tea to the sky and land (цацал) outside.

Who: you or a grandfather

09:0015 min

Brief the helpers, dress the child

  • Tell each helper their job (page 1). Give the gift-list helper the cutting order (last page).
  • If you hired an ерөөлч or a fiddle player, tell them the plan: when to play, how many tunes, when the long blessing comes.
  • Dress the child in his deel.

Who: you, mother

09:3030 min

Guests arrive

  • Greet guests at the door. Seat the elders in the place of honour: at the back of the room, facing the door (хоймор). Seat the first cutter in the best seat.
  • Serve milk tea. Ask quietly: "Ерөөл бэлдсэн үү?" Give a blessing ticket to anyone who did not prepare one.

Who: you and mother at the door; a helper serves tea

10:0010 min

Opening: welcome and airag

You say

Эрхэм хүндэт аав ээж, ах дүү, анд нөхөд минь, сайхан өглөөний мэнд хүргэе!

Өнөөдөр манай хүү хий насаараа хүрч, анхны сэвлэгээ үргээлгэх өлзий сайхан өдөр боллоо.

Энэ өдрийг бидэнтэй хамт тэмдэглэхээр ирсэн та бүхэнд гэр бүлийнхээ өмнөөс талархал илэрхийлье.

  • Offer the first bowl of airag to the oldest guest. Ask them for a short blessing.
You say

Найраа эхлэхийн өмнө уламжлалынхаа дагуу айргийнхаа дээжийг өргөе.

ахаа (эгчээ), найрын ерөөлөө дэвшүүлж өгөхийг хүсье.

If you have a fiddle player: one tune here.

10:1510 min

The first cut

  • The child sits at the head of the table, then stands up for the cut.
You say

Одоо найрын гол ёслол эхэлж байна.

Хүүхэдтэй ивээл жилтэй, буян хишигтэй ахаа (эгчээ), манай хүүгийн сэвлэгийг анх хөндөж, хайчны амыг нээж өгнө үү.

За одоо хүүгийнхээ даахийг үргээе!

  • The first cutter touches the hair with the wooden knife.
  • Then cuts a small lock at the right temple with the хадаг scissors. Mother takes the lock into the хадаг.
  • The child tastes milk from a cup. The first cutter dabs a little milk on the child's forehead.
  • The first cutter says the blessing (first-cutter card) and gives the gift.

Who: first cutter, mother, milk helper

10:2515 min

The family cuts

  • Order: grandparents, then mother and you, then the father's side and the mother's side (авга, нагац), oldest first.
  • When it is your turn, the stand-in MC says one line for you. Cut, say your blessing, and take the microphone back.
You say

Одоо өвөө, эмээ нараа урьж байна. Хүүгийнхээ сэвлэгт гар хүргэнэ үү.

Одоо авга, нагац ах дүү нараа урьж байна.

10:4035 min

The guests cut

  • Oldest guests first. Lead the child sunwise around the room, or let guests come to the child.
  • Each guest: sips milk from a small cup, cuts a tiny lock, says a blessing, puts the gift in the pouch. The gift helper writes down who gave what.
  • About 40 seconds per person. With about 40 guests, plan 35 minutes.
You say

Хүндэт зочид маань ахмадаасаа эхлээд нар зөв дарааллаар сэвлэгт гар хүргэнэ үү.

Сүүнээс амсаад, хадагтай хайчаар өчүүхэн хэсгийг хайчилж, ерөөлөө хэлээд, бэлгээ уутанд хийнэ үү.

Ерөөл бэлдээгүй бол ширээн дээр ерөөлийн хуудас бий.

If the child is tired or cries: stop for 5 minutes. Let him sit on his mother's lap, give him a toy or a snack, then continue. Do not force him.

You say (only if you need a break)

Манай хүү бага зэрэг амсхийе. Хэдэн минутын дараа үргэлжлүүлье.

11:1510 min

The long blessing closes the ritual

  • The ерөөлч or a respected elder says the long blessing, with music if you have a fiddle player. The cutting is now finished, before noon.
You say

Сэвлэг үргээх ёслолоо ерөөлөөр өндөрлүүлье.

ах маань сэвлэг үргээх ерөөл дэвшүүлнэ.

11:3060 min

The feast

  • Serve цагаалга to everyone first, and a small bowl to each child. Then the meal.
  • Airag for toasts. No vodka.
You say

Хүүгийнхээ сэвлэгийг үргээж, та бүхний ерөөл хишгийг хүртлээ.

Одоо цагаалгаа амсаж, найраа үргэлжлүүлье. Идээ ундаагаа сайхан хүртээрэй.

Who: kitchen helper serves; you sit with the elders

12:3030 min

Songs, photos, play

  • Songs at the table. Family photo with the child and the first cutter. Cake if you have one. The children play.
13:0015 min

Finish the haircut (optional now)

  • You shave or trim the rest of the hair with the clipper. Put all of the hair into the хадаг.
  • If the child is tired, do it later at home or at a barber. Ask the barber to give you all the hair.
13:3020 min

Thank-you gifts and closing speech

  • Give the special gift to the first cutter. Give the small gift bags to the guest children.
You say

Өнөөдөр манай хүүгийн сэвлэг үргээх найрт ирж, ерөөл бэлгээ хайрласан та бүхэнд гүн талархал илэрхийлье.

Хүүгийнхээ сэвлэгийг анх хөндсөн ахдаа (эгчдээ) тусгайлан баярласнаа хэлье.

Та бүхний ерөөсөн ерөөл биелж, бүгдээрээ эрүүл энх, сайн сайхан яваарай.

14:00and after

Guests leave, and after

  • See the elders to the door. If you wish, give them some of the food to take home.
  • Fold the хадаг with all the hair and keep it in a safe place, like the family chest. Never throw it away.
  • Keep the gift list. Call or message the elders and the first cutter the next day to thank them.

If something goes wrong

  • The child cries or is tired: pause, let him sit on his mother's lap, continue sitting down. Do not force him.
  • A guest comes after noon: traditionally they do not cut the hair; they join the meal. Your family can decide.
  • The first cutter cannot come: the oldest grandparent cuts first. If there is none, you (the father) cut first.
  • Not enough time: let a married couple or a family cut one after another quickly, each with a short blessing.

Based on Mongolian sources and parents' experiences (2016–2026). Customs differ by family, region and lama. Your elders and your lama have the last word.
