# Food, Drink and Table Setting for the Mongolian Child's First Haircut Ceremony (Үсний найр / Сэвлэг үргээх ёслол)

**In short:** In every source, the one food that must be on the table is **цагаалга**: aarts (dried curd) with raisins, plus white rice cooked with milk. Guests taste it first, and that starts the feast. The child is given milk from a cup. Traditional sources say **no vodka or wine**. Only airag (fermented mare's milk) is allowed, for the first toast and blessing. No source we could read says that ул боов (stacked biscuits) or ууц (sheep's back) is required for a haircut feast. Those belong to Tsagaan Sar (Lunar New Year) and weddings. Today many families in the city hold the feast in a restaurant. In 2024, prices ran from about 12,800–30,000₮ per person for buffets to 89,000–135,000₮ per person for set menus in event halls.

Research date: 2026-10-11. Note on method: the search tool is US-based and returns few Mongolian pages. Several key Mongolian pages could not be loaded (neguusel.com, mongolcom.mn, tusgal.mn, ikhmongol.mn, ntn.mn, mglnews.mn, washyoku.tavanbogd.mn). Where a fact comes only from a search-result snippet and not from a page I read, it is marked "(snippet only, page not loaded)".

## 1. Traditional table: which foods are required and which are optional?

### Takeaway
The only food that every source calls required ("заавал") is **цагаалга**: үзэмтэй аарц (aarts, a dried curd, with raisins) and сүүтэй цагаан будаа (white rice cooked with milk). Fresh food in general, milk, and airag come next. Meat and other dishes are served but are not named as required. No source we could read lists ул боов or ууц as part of the haircut feast.

### Cited Findings
- **Цагаалга is the one required food.** "Энэ найранд заавал байх ёстой идээний зүйл нь цагаалга юм." ("The food that must be at this feast is tsagaalga.") Everyone tastes it, and each child guest gets a bowl of it. Source: updown.mn, 2018-08-21, reprinted from the newspaper Тусгал. — [updown.mn](https://updown.mn/55761.html); same wording in [zindaa.mn, 2018-02-22](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn)
- **What цагаалга is made of:** "заавал байх зүйл нь цагаалга хэмээх үзэмтэй аарцтай, сүүтэй будаа болно." ("What must be there is tsagaalga, meaning aarts with raisins and rice with milk.") Source: arkhangai.mn, 2018-06-08. — [arkhangai.mn](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616)
- "цагаалаг үзэмтэй аарц, сүүтэй цагаан будаа заавал бэлдсэн байх нь чухал" ("It is important that white aarts with raisins and white rice with milk are prepared.") Source: Huree.mn, 2024-01-17. — [huree.mn](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/)
- Ikon.mn (2022-02-16, an advertorial for Говь мираж restaurant) says families prepare "идээ цагаа", and that you must prepare ("заавал бэлдэж") үзэмтэй аарц and сүүтэй цагаан будаа. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/2gs3)
- English sources agree: "especially curd with white raisins and rice with milk." — [Geru Travel](https://gerutravel.com/hair-cutting-ceremony-of-children/); "important to prepare food, especially white raisin curd and white rice with milk". The feast "begins with white rice". The ceremony includes "tsagaalga (dairy products) and meat". — [CorrectMongolia](https://correctmongolia.com/mongolian-haircutting-ceremony-4-fascinating-facts/)
- **Fresh food rule:** hosts serve "аль болох шинэ соргог идээ ундаа" (food and drink that are as fresh as possible). Hard spirits ("хатуу сархад") and old or leftover food ("хуучин идээ будаа") are never prepared. There are no other strict food bans. — [arkhangai.mn, 2018](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616); [Huree.mn table article, 2024-01-17](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%B0%D1%85%D1%8C-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D1%8B%D0%BD-%D1%88%D0%B8%D1%80%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D1%85/)
- **Битүүлэг (a closed, "sealed" dish):** the Huree.mn table article says whether to make it depends on local custom, and the family decides. — [Huree.mn, 2024-01-17](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%B0%D1%85%D1%8C-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D1%8B%D0%BD-%D1%88%D0%B8%D1%80%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D1%85/)
- **Table setting phrase:** families "ширээ засч, идээ будаа өргөөд" (set the table and offer food and grain dishes) before the child is seated. — [zindaa.mn, 2018](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn); [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/55761.html)
- **Meat:** CorrectMongolia lists meat among the foods but gives no details. — [CorrectMongolia](https://correctmongolia.com/mongolian-haircutting-ceremony-4-fascinating-facts/). A foreign guest's account mentions mutton soup, cherry juice, aaruul (dried curds) and boortsog (fried dough). The exact source is unclear. It is probably a 2014 guest blog — [laurenjavins.wordpress.com](https://laurenjavins.wordpress.com/2014/09/09/hair-cutting-ceremony/) (snippet only, page not loaded)
- **Sweets:** Huree.mn (2024) mentions чихэр амттан (candy and sweets) only as a modern way to please the children, not as a ritual food. — [Huree.mn food article, 2024-01-17](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D0%BD%D1%8B-%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%BE%D0%BB/)
- **Ул боов in general (not for haircuts):** ul boov is a layered biscuit stack that is central to the Tsagaan Sar table. "The number of layers ... represents the status of the family." — [Wikipedia: Ul boov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ul_boov)
- **No source we read puts ул боов or ууц on the haircut table.** None of these mention ул боов: Huree.mn (2024), zindaa.mn (2018), updown.mn (2018), arkhangai.mn (2018), ikon.mn (2022), Geru Travel or CorrectMongolia. Two searches that targeted this point also found no direct link between the haircut feast and ул боов. — [search result summary](https://ub.life/p/6585) (see Gaps)

### Inferences
- A practical classification, based on the sources above:
  - **Required:** цагаалга (үзэмтэй аарц + сүүтэй цагаан будаа); a cup of fresh milk for the child; fresh (not leftover) food.
  - **Strongly expected:** airag, for the first toast and blessing; other white foods (цагаан идээ: aaruul, cheese, butter, cream), which appear as the "дээж" gifts guests bring (see section 3).
  - **Optional / family choice:** a тавгийн идээ (ул боов stack); meat dishes (boiled mutton, бууз, хуушуур, банш, шөл); битүүлэг; candy and sweets; cake; salads and fruit.
- Бууз, хуушуур and boiled mutton are standard Mongolian feast dishes, so families who hold the feast at home very likely serve them. But no haircut-specific source names them. They are "normal feast food", not "haircut ritual food".
- Ууц (the whole roasted or boiled sheep's back) is not mentioned for haircuts in any source found. It is most likely not a standard part of үсний найр. The traditional sheep at a haircut is the **gift** to the child (a white or "халзан цагаан" sheep or lamb), not a dish. — gift noted in [zindaa.mn](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn) and [Huree.mn](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D0%BD%D1%8B-%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%BE%D0%BB/)

### Gaps
- A search-result summary claimed that at a haircut, "Цагаан идээний тавганд бүх төрлийн цагаан идээ, ул боов, боорцог, айраг тавьдаг. Ахмадыг хүндэтгэн гурав буюу түүнээс дээш боовыг давхарлан тавьдаг" ("The white-food plate holds all kinds of dairy, ul boov, boortsog and airag. To honour elders, three or more layers of boov are stacked"). I could **not** trace this to a page I could open. The likely sources (ene.mn, washyoku.tavanbogd.mn, mglnews.mn) failed to load (404, certificate error, socket error). Treat it as **unverified**.
- No source gives the dairy items one by one for a haircut table (ааруул, өрөм, ээзгий, бяслаг, шар тос). Only "цагаан идээ" in general is mentioned.
- No ethnographic source on ууц at үсний найр was found, for or against.

## 2. How the table is arranged (тавгийн идээ, layers, what goes on top, place of honour, хоймор)

### Takeaway
There is no haircut-specific rule for the table layout. The general Mongolian rules for тавгийн идээ apply if a family makes one: an **odd number of layers** (3, 5, 7, 9), set by the household's place in the family line, with butter and cream on top. Seating has clear rules: the child sits at the head of the table, and the person with the child's "ивээл жил" (protector year in the 12-year animal cycle) sits in the honoured north-west seat.

### Cited Findings
- **Odd layers:** ул боов (хавсай боов) is stacked "гурав, тав, долоо, ес давхарладаг ёстой" (must be stacked in 3, 5, 7 or 9 layers). Source: ikon.mn, 2014-01-30, citing budda.mn. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3vb)
- **Layers by household:** a grandfather's home ("өвгийн гэр") uses **7** layers. A father's home ("эцгийн гэр") uses **5**. A married son's home ("ураглаж хуримласан хүүгийн гэр") uses **3**. — [ikon.mn, 2014](https://ikon.mn/n/3vb); same rule in [ub.life, 2017-02-23](https://ub.life/p/6585), which adds that a newly married young couple may also use 3 layers.
- **Nine layers are only for very large events:** nine layers are for state ceremonies and big public wedding feasts. An ordinary household making nine layers on its own is seen as a mistake. — [ub.life, 2017](https://ub.life/p/6585)
- **Meaning of the numbers:** 9 = heaven, 7 = earth, 3 = the origin of the world. — [ub.life, 2017](https://ub.life/p/6585). Another explanation: 3 layers = the three stones of the hearth (тулгын гурван чулуу) / "happiness-suffering-happiness"; 5 layers = the five elements (таван бие махбодь). — [neguusel.com](https://neguusel.com/9615/) (snippet only, page not loaded)
- **The name "ул боов":** "ул" (sole of the foot) carries the meaning of "going forward / moving ahead". — [neguusel.com](https://neguusel.com/9615/) (snippet only, from the page title)
- **What goes on top:** whatever the number of layers, the top of the main plate is decorated with cream and butter (өрөм, тос). — [ub.life, 2017](https://ub.life/p/6585). The big plate is decorated with ул боов, самар (sugar lumps / candy), сүүл (fat tail), цоохор боов, саран боорцог, "шар, цагаан тос, ааруул хурууд, бал бурам, чихэр", үзэм, чавга (raisins, prunes). — [ikon.mn, 2014](https://ikon.mn/n/3vb)
- **Tsagaan Sar only:** one article says еэвэн (a type of thin pastry) is not placed on top of the тавгийн идээ. — [shuud.mn](https://www.shuud.mn/a/463951) (title only, not read)
- **Seating at the haircut:** the child is dressed and seated "ширээний тэргүүнд" (at the head of the table). — [zindaa.mn, 2018](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn); [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/55761.html); [CorrectMongolia](https://correctmongolia.com/mongolian-haircutting-ceremony-4-fascinating-facts/)
- "Гэрийн баруун хойно хүүхдийн ивээл жилтэй хүн сууна." (The person with the child's protector year sits in the north-west of the ger.) This marks them as the most honoured guest. — [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/); [zindaa.mn, 2018](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn)
- The father, grandfather or "найрын тэргүүн" (head of the feast) opens the ceremony. — [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/55761.html); [Geru Travel](https://gerutravel.com/hair-cutting-ceremony-of-children/)
- **Timing:** the table is set in the morning or before noon of a chosen lucky day ("өглөөгүүр буюу үдээс өмнө"). — [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/). Guests who arrive after noon do not touch the child's hair. — [arkhangai.mn, 2018](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616)

### Inferences
- If a young family (parents of the child, usually a "married son's home") makes a тавгийн идээ for the haircut, the household rule points to **3 layers**. If the feast is held at the grandparents' home, 5 or 7 layers. This applies the general rule; no source confirms it for haircuts specifically.
- The тавгийн идээ, if used, would naturally go in the хоймор (the honoured back part of the ger or room), near the ивээл жилтэй person and the child at the head of the table. This follows normal Mongolian table etiquette. No haircut source states it.

### Gaps
- No source describes a haircut-specific table plan (where цагаалга, the milk cup, airag and the тавгийн идээ stand on the table).
- No source compares the haircut layer count with the Tsagaan Sar layer count. The rules above come from Tsagaan Sar sources.

## 3. What is offered first, and the custom of "дээж" (first portion) for elders and the child

### Takeaway
The feast opens with **цагаалга**, which everyone tastes. Sources also say airag is raised first, with a blessing ("эхэлж айраг сөн барьж"). The person who cuts the hair tastes цагаалга or is given milk tea or airag. The child is given milk from a cup. Guests may bring "дээж" (first portions) of white food on plates.

### Cited Findings
- Guests "заавал амсаж найрыг эхлүүлдэг" (must taste [the tsagaalga] to start the feast). — [ikon.mn, 2022](https://ikon.mn/n/2gs3); [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/)
- "даахь үргээх найранд эхэлж айраг сөн барьж, ерөөлч бэлэг дэмбэрэлтэй үг хэлж ерөөдөг ёстой." (At a haircut feast, airag is offered first, and the blessing-giver ("ерөөлч") speaks words of good fortune.) — [arkhangai.mn, 2018-06-08](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616)
- "даахь үргээх хүнд цагаалга амсуулдаг" (the person who cuts the hair is given tsagaalga to taste). — search snippet tied to [mnb.mn](https://www.mnb.mn/i/149328) / [zogii.mn](https://zogii.mn/newsm/8/2/157196) (snippet only, page not loaded)
- The person giving each haircut is offered "a drink of milk tea (haarum) or, in some cases, fermented mare's milk (aireg)". That person then offers the drink to the child. Source: an American visitor's blog, 2014. — [drvalfarmer.blogspot.com](http://drvalfarmer.blogspot.com/2014/02/mongolian-haricutting-ceremony.html)
- Order given by updown.mn / Тусгал (2018): set the table and offer food → the child sits at the head → scissors are purified with juniper and incense (арц, хүж) → hair is cut starting from the right side → the child tastes milk → blessing → the child is led sunwise ("нар зөв") past parents, elders and guests, and each cuts some hair → bowls of tsagaalga / rice are given to all. — [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/55761.html)
- **Дээж:** "Ер нь гэрийн дээж, найрын дээж гэж хоёр таваг цагаан идээ буюу ондоон бэлэгтэй ирнэ." (Usually [guests] come with two plates of white food, a "home дээж" and a "feast дээж", or with a gift.) — search snippet (snippet only; source page among [mnb.mn](https://www.mnb.mn/i/149328), [ikhmongol.mn](http://ikhmongol.mn/show/5107), [mongoltoli.mn](https://mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/29134), none loaded)

### Inferences
- A workable order of serving, combining the sources: (1) airag or milk is raised with the opening blessing; (2) the ивээл жилтэй guest and haircutter taste цагаалга or milk tea; (3) the child gets milk after the cut; (4) every guest and child gets a bowl of цагаалга / milk rice; (5) the main meal follows (tea, meat dishes, etc.).
- Sources differ on what comes "first" (цагаалга vs airag). This is probably a local or family difference. Both are white or dairy foods, so both fit the "white = pure, good start" idea.

### Gaps
- No source describes "дээж өргөх" (offering the first portion) to elders at a haircut in detail, for example who gets the first cup of tea.
- The two-plate "гэрийн дээж / найрын дээж" custom is from a snippet only and could not be checked.

## 4. Is milk used in the ritual itself? (brief; the main ritual is covered by another researcher)

### Takeaway
Yes. All sources say the child is given milk to taste from a cup during or after the cut. One foreign blog says milk is rubbed into the child's hair before each lock is cut.

### Cited Findings
- "аягатай сүүнээс хүүхдэд амсуулж" (the child is given milk to taste from a cup). — [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/55761.html); [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/)
- The person with the lucky zodiac year "tasted the cup of milk" and gave a wish to the child. — [Geru Travel](https://gerutravel.com/hair-cutting-ceremony-of-children/)
- "Milk is also rubbed into the child's hair before each lock is cut." — [drvalfarmer.blogspot.com, 2014](http://drvalfarmer.blogspot.com/2014/02/mongolian-haricutting-ceremony.html)
- The scissors are purified with juniper and incense smoke (not milk). — [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%81%D1%8D%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D1%91%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BB/)

### Inferences
- A cup of fresh milk must be on the table near the child. It is a ritual item, not only a drink.

### Gaps
- No Mongolian source I read mentions butter (тос) on the child's head. Rubbing milk on the hair comes from a single foreign blog.

## 5. Alcohol customs: vodka, airag, toasts, and the alcohol-free trend

### Takeaway
The traditional rule is clear: **no vodka or wine ("архи дарс ордоггүй")** at a haircut feast. Airag (and other dairy drinks) may be offered for the first toast and blessing. The stated reason is respect for the child's "four divine qualities". Writers since 2018 criticise haircut feasts that turn into adult drinking parties. Some city restaurants still let families bring their own drinks.

### Cited Findings
- "найранд архи дарс ордоггүй" (vodka and wine do not enter the feast). Instead, airag is offered first with a blessing. — [arkhangai.mn, 2018-06-08](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616) (same article reprinted at [dayarmongol.mn](http://dayarmongol.mn/index.php?newsid=7086))
- "Даахь үргээх ёслолын үеэр архи дарс хэрэглэдэггүй." The reason: the ceremony honours four divine qualities of a small child: no greed, no hoarding, no sense of "dirty", and no telling high from low. — [updown.mn, "Хүүхдийн үсний найр томчуудын 'архидах'-ын шалтаг биш", 2018-06-08](https://updown.mn/50592.html)
- "архи дарс хэрэглэдэггүй ... малын шим айргаа сөгнөж болох" (no vodka or wine; airag, a product of the herd, may be offered). — [Huree.mn table article, 2024-01-17](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%B0%D1%85%D1%8C-%D2%AF%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8D%D1%8D%D1%85-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D1%8B%D0%BD-%D1%88%D0%B8%D1%80%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%B3-%D1%85/)
- "Хүүхдийн үсний найр бол томчуудын найрын шалтаг биш." (A child's haircut feast is not an excuse for an adults' party.) The article criticises inviting all relatives, friends and co-workers. — [arkhangai.mn, 2018](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616)
- Public figure Kh. Khurelbaatar on X (about August 2020): "Архи дарс амсаж хэрэглэхгүйг анхаар!" (Be careful not to drink vodka or wine!). He also says not everyone should be invited. — [X post](https://x.com/KhKhurelbaatar/status/1296622434720702464)
- **Modern restaurant practice:** A&G Entertainment Restaurant lets guests bring drinks from outside ("drinks may be brought from outside"). Huree.mn notes this as a cheaper option. — [Huree.mn venues article, 2024-01-17](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%82%D3%A9%D1%80%D1%81%D3%A9%D0%BD-%D3%A9%D0%B4%D3%A9%D1%80-%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80-%D1%85%D0%B8%D0%B9%D1%85%D1%8D/); [Huree.mn food article](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80%D0%BD%D1%8B-%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%BE%D0%BB/)

### Inferences
- The repeated articles (2018, 2020, 2024) warning against alcohol suggest that many modern haircut parties **do** serve vodka. The "alcohol-free haircut feast" is both the traditional rule and a modern push by commentators.
- For a family that wants to follow tradition: offer airag (or milk / milk tea) for toasts, and serve juice and soft drinks for the meal.

### Gaps
- No survey or data on how many families serve alcohol today.
- No source found on toast etiquette at haircut feasts beyond "airag first, with a blessing".

## 7. Food the hosts send home with guests (идээний дээж, return packs)

### Takeaway
I found **no source** that describes food or return packs that hosts give to guests at a haircut feast. The sources describe the opposite direction: guests bring gifts (and possibly plates of white food) to the child.

### Cited Findings
- Guests give the child gifts. Traditionally this was a white sheep or lamb. Now it is toys, stationery, hair bands, badges, "чихэр боов" (candy and biscuits) and money. — [zindaa.mn, 2018](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn); [Geru Travel](https://gerutravel.com/hair-cutting-ceremony-of-children/)
- Each child guest gets a bowl of цагаалга / rice to eat at the feast ("аяга, аягаар өгч будаалдаг"). — [zindaa.mn, 2018](https://news.zindaa.mn/2adn)
- The 2014 blog describes nothing that guests take home. — [drvalfarmer.blogspot.com](http://drvalfarmer.blogspot.com/2014/02/mongolian-haricutting-ceremony.html)

### Inferences
- In general Mongolian feast etiquette, hosts often give guests a portion of food to take home. Modern parties may give small candy bags to child guests. But I found no source for this at haircut feasts. The report writer should not state it as fact.

### Gaps
- "идээний дээж" or "өглөг" given to guests at a haircut: no source found.
- Modern "return gift" or favor bags for haircut parties: no source found (probably on social media only).

## 8. Quantities: how much food for 30, 50 or 100 guests

### Takeaway
I found **no source** that gives food amounts per guest for a haircut feast. Restaurants price per person, so they handle quantity. Traditional sources stress a **small** feast for close family, not a large one.

### Cited Findings
- Traditionally the feast is small: "олон зочин урихгүй" (do not invite many guests). Only close relatives and the child's protector-year people come. — [arkhangai.mn, 2018](http://arkhangai.mn/index.php?newsid=3616); [updown.mn, 2018](https://updown.mn/50592.html)
- Venue sizes show typical modern guest numbers: 30–120 (Altai Grill), 50–80 (La Sirius), up to 75 (Чин Ван Ханддоржийн өргөө), up to 100–150 (A&G, Monte, Park Castle). — [Huree.mn, 2024](https://huree.mn/%D1%85%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%85%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%82%D3%A9%D1%80%D1%81%D3%A9%D0%BD-%D3%A9%D0%B4%D3%A9%D1%80-%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80-%D1%85%D0%B8%D0%B9%D1%85%D1%8D/); [mmarket.mn](https://mmarket.mn/en/%D2%AF%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%80-%D1%85%D0%B8%D0%B9%D1%85-%D0%B3%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%80/)
- A Ukrainian caterer suggests about 3 sweet portions per guest for a candy table. This is not a Mongolian source; use only as a loose guide. — [Dzyga Catering](https://dzyga-catering.com.ua/ua/stati/shho-take-kendi-bar/)

### Inferences
- Modern city haircut feasts commonly have 30–150 guests, judging by venue sizes. That is much larger than the traditional "close family only" feast.
- For цагаалга, each guest only needs a small taste and each child a small bowl. So the amount is small compared with the main meal. No source gives grams or litres.

### Gaps
- No source on kg of meat, number of бууз, litres of milk or airag, or amount of цагаалга per guest. A home-cooking guide or caterer quote would be needed.
