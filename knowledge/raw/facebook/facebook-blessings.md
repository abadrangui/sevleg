- Taboo: don't throw hair carelessly ("Буянд цөв суулгаж, муу ёр дуудна"); birds like magpies use it for nests -> misfortune; no alcohol; don't let strangers touch.
- Ends with contrarian claim: keeping hair in хадаг/pouch was "a big mistake" because "keeping hair collects illness" (text cut).

### G. "DREAM DAY kids event center" page (business, Aug 15 2022) https://www.facebook.com/dreamdaykids/posts/pfbid0HTUVFKVyt3oGAeXygBmvRCaTyK66VCGvre8wTf1saBX41zmDk1eHsdG4deoa4i6ql
- Long traditional text (encyclopedic). GUEST ETIQUETTE: "Найрт уригдсан хүмүүс заавал бага ч гэсэн бэлэгтэй ирнэ" – bring two plates of white food: "гэрийн дээж" (for the household, like a normal visit) and "найрын дээж" (for the feast), or a cake/ondoon gift; for the child: clothes, shoes, silk, cloth, coins, or promise a sheep or foal.
- Arrive EARLY morning ("өглөө эртлэн очих ёстой") – scissors before noon. Some areas avoid Thursday ("хүний хишиг өдөр").
- Younger siblings: same age as the eldest child's ceremony. Sickly (энхэр) children: only a token trim at 6–7 or 8–9.
- Tray (silver or wooden) with five grains (таван тариа) + red & white cloth layers + scissors on top. Father holds the tray; mother holds bowl of milk + plate of white food; parents lead the child to the ивээл elder, offer white food: "хүүгийн минь (охины минь) даахийг үргээж хайрла", child bows. Elder tastes, takes scissors, cuts one lock: boy right temple (санчиг), girl left.
G (continued, full):
- First cutter / ерөөлч takes дээж from the plate of white food, anoints (мялаах) the hair, says "даахины ерөөл" = short бэлгэ дэмбэрлийн үг, e.g. right side -> lotus, left side -> knowledge, crown -> wisdom.
- Scissors with BLUE and WHITE хадаг tied on handle; cut a lock from the forehead side, wrap in хадаг (kept as remedy). Child tastes milk; wish: "урт настай, удаан жаргалтай, аавдаа ачлалтай, ээждээ энэрэлтэй, төр улсдаа зүтгэлтэй, түмэн олныхоо манлай болж яваарай".
- Then child led sunwise past father, mother, авга, нагац, elders, guests: EVERYONE cuts a bit and MUST say a blessing ("ерөөл бэлгэтэй үг заавал хэлж"); hair added to хадаг bundle.
- Gifts: old – "толгойг мялааж" promise a white-headed (халзан цагаан) sheep or lamb; modern – toys, pencils, stationery, hair ribbons, badges, sweets, money.
- Boys keep a "тэвэг" tuft (back of head, "баатар хуйх" not touched by blade), girls two "хөхөл" tufts.
- Feast: цагаалга must; songs & fiddle; kids wrestle, foal races. Hair braided after (girls) with camel-wool thread.
